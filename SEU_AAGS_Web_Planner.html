Summer courses

Registering for a summer course: Summer courses are registered through JICS only, not through the CC bookstore. Check SEU's academic calendar for the date spring and summer registration opens.

Summer courses are stand-alone SEU online courses. They use SEU curriculum, professors and assignments, and do not align with CC strand courses.

How the summer is structured: Session A is the first 8 weeks; Session B is the second 8 weeks. A summer is 16 weeks, so a student can take two courses in one summer. MATH 1213 is the exception: it runs the full 16 weeks.

The earliest summer term is the one following Challenge II. There is no summer term before Challenge II begins.
